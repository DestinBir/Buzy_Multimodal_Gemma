# Buzy AI

**AI-Powered Document Intelligence for African Organizations**

Buzy AI transforms messy business documents — contracts, invoices, meeting recordings, and reports — into structured, explainable decisions using Google's Gemma 4 model.

Built for the **GDG on Campus UCB — Build with Gemma 2026 Hackathon**.

## How to use

1. Upload documents (PDF, DOCX, TXT), images (receipts, dashboards), or audio (meeting recordings)
2. Ask a business question in natural language
3. Get a structured analysis with evidence citations

## Example questions

- "What are the key obligations and risks in this contract?"
- "Summarize this invoice and flag any anomalies."
- "Which supplier represents the highest operational risk?"

## Note

This Space loads a fine-tuned Gemma 4 model (~5B parameters). First inference may take 1-2 minutes while the model loads on the GPU.
